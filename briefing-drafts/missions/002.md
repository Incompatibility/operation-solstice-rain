# Situation

Following the initial deployment to Nov Elysia,
Union forces are regrouping at FOB Saber.

Hostile Vestan mechanized elements continue to
operate throughout the city.

Captain Farris and Ambassador Nilan Bannerjee
remain the priority of the recovery operation.

- Locate the missing Union personnel.
- Establish a safe extraction route.
- Recover the designated personnel.
- Return to friendly-controlled territory.

# Stakes

- Prolonged operations increase exposure to
  hostile mechanized forces.
